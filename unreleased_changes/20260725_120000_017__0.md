* Feature : Support the openCypher `CASE` expression in graph queries, both the simple form (`CASE x WHEN v THEN r ... ELSE e END`) and the searched form (`CASE WHEN <condition> THEN r ... ELSE e END`), including conditions combined with `AND`/`OR`/`NOT` and `IS [NOT] NULL`. A missing `ELSE` yields null. Usable anywhere a value expression is allowed (e.g. `RETURN`, function arguments, arithmetic).
