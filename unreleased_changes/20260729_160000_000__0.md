* Bug : Roll back rather than commit a failed graph retention pass or merge. A failure part-way through retention's property-index rebuild durably committed a cleared or half-rebuilt index, after which property-anchored queries silently returned no rows; a fragment refused as corrupt had already had all of its nodes committed without their edges, because the consistency check ran after the first write. Retention and merge now validate before writing where they can and abort their transaction on any failure.
