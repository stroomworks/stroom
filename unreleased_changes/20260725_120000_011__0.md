* Feature : Support arithmetic operators (`+`, `-`, `*`, `/`, `^`, with precedence and grouping parentheses) in Cypher `RETURN`/`WITH` expressions, e.g. `RETURN a.balance * 1.2`, `(a.hi - a.lo) / 2`. Evaluated by Stroom's expression engine (no runtime change); a computed operand in a `MATCH` `WHERE` and the `%` operator are not yet supported.
