* Feature : Support a single `MATCH ... WITH ... [WHERE] RETURN ...` pipe in Cypher, enabling aggregate-then-filter (Cypher's `HAVING`), e.g. `MATCH (p:Person {id:'p1'})-[:PARTY_TO]->(c:Crime) WITH p.id AS pid, count(c) AS n WHERE n > 1 RETURN pid, n`. The `WITH`'s `WHERE` filters the projected/aggregated columns, and references after the `WITH` are scope-checked against its columns (an out-of-scope reference fails at compile). v1 requires aliased `WITH` items and rejects a second `MATCH` after the `WITH`, `ORDER BY`/`SKIP`/`LIMIT` on the pipe, aggregates in the final `RETURN`, and `WITH` combined with `DIFF`/`RETURN GRAPH`.
